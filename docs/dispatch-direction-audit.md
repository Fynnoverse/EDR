# Dispatch direction audit — 2026-09-07

Directions describe the side where a route leaves the in-game dispatch panel.
The vertical position of a horizontal branch does not make it an up/down exit.
Do not infer a panel's orientation from coordinates or the neighbouring panel.

## Confirmed corrections

- Łowicz Główny: Jackowice right; Bednary and its intermediate PZS points left.
  Source: user's in-game observation. Other branches and separate PZS viewpoints
  still require a readable panel reference.
- Zduńska Wola: Karsznice and Dionizów exit left, alongside Gajewniki.
- Zgierz: Marysin exits left; the Kontrewers branch exits right, alongside Glinnik.
  Intermediate timetable points retain the side of their branch.
